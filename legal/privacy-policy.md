# Privacy Policy — FASTag Chain Reaction

**Effective date:** [INSERT DATE]
**Last updated:** [INSERT DATE]

We built FASTag Chain Reaction to make tolls smoother for you — fewer surprises at the plaza, smarter recharges, and a clearer picture of what you actually spend on highways. To do that, we need a few pieces of information about you and your vehicle. This page explains, in plain English, exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what control you have. If anything here is unclear, write to our grievance officer (details at the bottom).

## 1. What we collect and exactly why

We only ask for data when a feature genuinely needs it. Here is the full list:

| Data | Why we need it |
|------|----------------|
| **Phone number** | To create your account, send OTPs, and recover access if you lose your phone. |
| **Vehicle registration number** | To look up your linked FASTag, fetch your balance, and match toll plaza pricing to your vehicle class (car vs. SUV vs. commercial). |
| **GPS location (only while you are driving)** | To detect when you are approaching a toll plaza within 5 km, so we can warn you of low balance and pre-fetch the right toll price. The moment you park or close the app, we stop collecting. You can wipe your location history with one tap inside Settings → Privacy. |
| **FASTag balance** | To show your current balance and warn you before a toll if you are running low. Fetched read-only from NPCI's NETC system. |
| **UPI handle (VPA)** | To let you top up your FASTag instantly without leaving the app. We never see or store your UPI PIN — that stays in your bank's UPI app. |
| **Transaction history (toll debits + recharges)** | To give you monthly toll summaries, GST-ready expense reports for business vehicles, and dispute support if a plaza overcharges you. |
| **Push notification token** | To send you low-balance alerts and toll-deduction confirmations. |

We do **not** collect: your contacts, your photos, your microphone, your other apps, your card numbers, your CVV, your UPI PIN, your Aadhaar, or your bank account number.

## 2. Who we share data with

We share the minimum data needed, with these parties only:

- **NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India)** — receives your vehicle registration number and FASTag tag ID to return your balance and process recharges. This is required for the FASTag system to work.
- **Your UPI app (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, your bank app, etc.)** — receives a payment request when you choose to recharge. Your UPI PIN never touches our servers.
- **NHAI and state highway authorities** — receive **only aggregated, anonymized** traffic and queue-length data (e.g., "Plaza X had average wait time of 4 minutes between 6–7 PM"). They never see your name, phone, vehicle, or individual trips.
- **Cloud infrastructure providers in India** — host our servers under strict contracts.
- **Nobody else.** We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, insurers, employers, or marketers.

If a court order or lawful government request forces us to share specific user data, we will comply with the minimum necessary, and we will notify you unless the order legally prevents us from doing so.

## 3. How long we keep your data

- **GPS pings:** automatically deleted after **24 hours**, unless they have already been aggregated into anonymized traffic telemetry.
- **Anonymized telemetry:** stripped of all personal identifiers within **7 days** of collection.
- **Transactions and recharges:** kept for **7 years** to comply with Income Tax and GST record-keeping requirements.
- **Account profile (phone, vehicle reg):** kept until you delete your account, then wiped within 30 days (except items the law requires us to retain).
- **Backups:** purged within 90 days of the original deletion.

## 4. Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act gives you the following rights, and we honor every one of them:

- **Right to access** — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you. We respond within 30 days.
- **Right to correction** — Fix any inaccurate or outdated information directly in the app or by writing to us.
- **Right to erasure** — Delete your account and personal data. We will keep only what law (tax records, fraud-prevention obligations) requires.
- **Right to data portability** — Download your transaction history and account data in a machine-readable format (JSON/CSV).
- **Right to withdraw consent** — Turn off any data collection at any time inside Settings → Privacy. Some features will stop working (e.g., turning off GPS disables toll proximity alerts), but core account functions will continue.
- **Right to nominate** — Appoint someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you cannot.
- **Right to grievance redressal** — Contact our grievance officer below. If unsatisfied, you can escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 5. Security

We use industry-standard protections: TLS 1.3 for all data in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, hardware-backed key management for sensitive fields, and least-privilege access for our engineers. No system is perfectly secure, but if we ever detect a breach affecting your data, we will notify the Data Protection Board within 72 hours and notify you without undue delay.

## 6. Children

FASTag Chain Reaction is for users **18 years and older**. A valid driving license is required to use any features tied to vehicle operation. If we learn that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it immediately.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we materially change what we collect or how we use it, we will notify you in-app at least 30 days before the change takes effect, and we will ask for fresh consent where the law requires it.

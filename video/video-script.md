## Voice-Over Notes

- **Accent:** clear, neutral Indian-English. Think NDTV / The Ken podcast, not BBC, not American.
- **Gender:** either works; recommend male voice in lower register for the tense opening, but a female VO would differentiate from typical fintech videos.
- **Pace:** ~158 wpm overall (per-segment 135–171 wpm). The script is engineered for 150 words across 57s of speaking time + 3s opening silence; do not rush, but do not drag the middle either.
- **Tone progression:** matter-of-fact (0:03–0:15) → curious, almost a reveal (0:15–0:30) → confident and warm (0:30–0:50) → quietly proud (0:50–0:58) → direct and grounded (0:58–1:00).
- **Delivery cues:**
  - "It's not the driver." → hit "not" firmly. Half-beat pause after.
  - "before it ever starts." → ease off; almost a whisper.
  - "one tap." → crisp, clipped.
  - "Pilot with us." → look-them-in-the-eye conviction. No upspeak.
- **Booth:** record dry, 48kHz, 24-bit. Apply light de-ess and a -3dB high-shelf at 8kHz in post.
- **Suggested talent platforms:** Voices.com (search "Indian English neutral"), Fiverr Pro tier only, or local Mumbai/Bangalore voice agency for highest authenticity.

## Subtitles / Captioning

- **Every line of VO has burnt-in captions.** LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Instagram autoplay muted — assume 85% of viewers never hear the audio.
- **Style:** white text, 90% opacity black pill background, sans-serif (Inter or Söhne), bottom-third safe area.
- **Timing:** word-level sync. Use Descript, CapCut auto-caption, or Premiere's Speech-to-Text — then hand-correct.
- **Two caption tracks to deliver:**
  1. Burnt-in (open captions) for social cuts.
  2. Separate `.srt` sidecar file for YouTube CC and accessibility.
- **On-screen text vs captions:** when an on-screen text card is showing (e.g., "1 car → 200+ stuck"), pause captions for that beat to avoid visual clutter.

## Recording Tips

### In-car shoot (Honda City, MH-12 plate)
- **Time of day:** 6:00–7:30 AM. Soft light, minimal traffic, no harsh windshield glare.
- **Route:** Khalapur toll, Mumbai-Pune expressway (eastbound entry from Mumbai side gives the best queue angle into morning light). Backup: Vashi toll, easier permits.
- **Camera:** Sony A7IV or iPhone 15 Pro Max in ProRes Log. GoPro Hero 12 on suction mount for dashboard POV.
- **Phone mount:** vent-mount or dashboard suction. Frame so the phone screen occupies the right third; driver hand visible on wheel left of frame.
- **Screen capture:** do NOT film the phone screen directly — moiré and reflections will ruin it. Screen-record on the phone, composite in post over a clean dashboard plate.
- **Safety + legal:** driver must NOT be the operator. Passenger shoots only. Do not stop on the expressway shoulder. Permits required for tripod use at toll plaza — contact MEPL (Mumbai-Pune toll operator) at least 7 days in advance.

### Drone shoot
- DGCA: Khalapur falls in a yellow zone — file NPNT plan via DigitalSky portal 48 hours ahead.
- Altitude cap: 120m AGL. Stay 30m+ from the toll plaza canopy.
- Golden hour, sun behind camera (west-facing for sunrise, east-facing for sunset).
- Backup: licensed stock from ShutterStock/Pond5 if permits fail.

### B-roll capture list (shoot 3× what you need)
- [ ] FASTag barrier closing/opening — 5 angles, 10s each
- [ ] MH-12 number plate close-up — 3s, sharp focus
- [ ] Driver hand on steering wheel, watch visible — 5s
- [ ] Honda City exterior tracking shot — 10s
- [ ] Phone notification animation in-car — 8s (re-shoot if light changes)
- [ ] Toll booth attendant (face blurred / silhouette only) — 5s
- [ ] Cars accelerating away from barrier (smooth flow) — 8s
- [ ] Wide expressway empty road shot for endcard backup — 10s
- [ ] Sunrise/sunset glow over expressway — 15s for transitions

### Audio capture in-car
- Lavalier mic on driver if any in-car dialogue (none in this script, but capture room tone for 30s).
- Boom outside for engine + horn ambience — phantom-powered shotgun on a 3m cable.
